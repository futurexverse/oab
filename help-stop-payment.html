Stop Payment on a Check

← Back to Help · Everything you need to know

When to Request One

A stop payment prevents a specific check from clearing.

• The check was lost or stolen
• You no longer want to pay the payee
• The amount on the check is incorrect
• The check was never delivered to the recipient

How to Request

Place the request before the check is presented for payment.

• Call 1-800-432-1000 (24/7) for the fastest response
• Provide the check number, exact amount and payee name
• You can also request one at a financial center
• Have your account details ready to speed things up

Fees and Duration

Stop payments last up to six months and can be renewed.

• Standard fee: $30.00 per check
• Fee may be waived for account holders in Preferred Rewards
• The stop expires automatically after six months
• Confirm the check hasn't already cleared before you request

Good to Know

If the check has already been paid, a stop payment can't undo it. Recurring transfers set up in Online Banking can be cancelled anytime without a fee.

Need Help?

Call 1-800-432-1000 (24/7) or visit the Help & Support page for more options.
